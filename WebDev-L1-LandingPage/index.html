Four indicators, one ruleset

Pulse is not a bundle of unrelated tools. Every layer reads off the same BBMA logic, so what you see on the 5-minute chart agrees with what you see on the 4-hour.

Trend bands

A moving-average envelope that widens and tightens with volatility, marking where price is extended versus fair.

Ribbon confluence

Fast and slow ribbons highlight only when they stack in order — the rest of the time they stay quiet on purpose.

Signal alerts

Push and webhook alerts fire on confirmed confluence, not on every crossover, so your phone isn't buzzing all session.

Multi-timeframe sync

Check the higher timeframe bias without leaving your chart — Pulse pulls it into a small panel in the corner.
